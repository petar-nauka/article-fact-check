# Green Hydrogen in the EU: Investment, Regulation and Real Capacity 2024-2026

In 2024, the European Union recorded a 35% increase in installed electrolyser capacity for clean hydrogen, reaching a total of 1.2 GW of operational capacity. This jump is driven mainly by the funding mechanism of the European Hydrogen Bank.

> **Factual comment -- C0001 (Partly Supported):** Operational capacity is conflated with the total project pipeline. **Recommended edit:** Replace “reaching a total of 1.2 GW of operational capacity” with “reaching about 580 MW of operational capacity, with more than 1.2 GW under construction”. **Evidence:** [S001](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-hydrogen-review-2024).

According to a report by the International Energy Agency (IEA), the average production cost of green hydrogen in Europe fell to EUR 2.50 per kilogram at the end of 2024. This makes green hydrogen cheaper than hydrogen produced from natural gas.

> **Factual comment -- C0003 (Contradicted):** Understated by more than half compared with actual market levels. **Recommended edit:** Replace “fell to EUR 2.50 per kilogram” with “ranges between EUR 5.50 and 7.80 per kilogram, depending on electricity prices”. **Evidence:** [S001](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-hydrogen-review-2024).

> **Factual comment -- C0004 (Contradicted):** Factually wrong comparison that is highly misleading. **Recommended edit:** Replace the claim with: “However, without subsidies green hydrogen still costs about twice as much as hydrogen from natural gas.” **Evidence:** [S001](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-hydrogen-review-2024).
